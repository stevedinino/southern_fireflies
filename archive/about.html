About Southern Fireflies Retreats

Southern Fireflies Retreats is a warm, creative getaway for crafters who want to relax, make, and connect. We host curated weekends with plenty of table space, good company, and time to dive deep into your projects.

Questions or special requests? We're happy to help — reach out anytime during registration.
