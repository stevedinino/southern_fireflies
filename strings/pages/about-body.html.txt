<h1>About Southern Fireflies Retreats</h1>
<p>Southern Fireflies Retreats is a warm, creative getaway for crafters who want to relax, make, and connect. We host curated weekends with plenty of table space, good company, and time to dive deep into your projects.</p>
<p>Questions or special requests? We're happy to help — reach out anytime during registration.</p>
