<b>How this works:</b> Choose an amount, tell us who it's for, and submit the form below. You'll
get a printable certificate right away, plus a backup copy by email. A <b>Merch</b> certificate
never expires; a <b>Retreat</b> certificate is good toward a specific upcoming retreat. Payment
isn't automatic here &mdash; please send the amount via Venmo or PayPal (details below, once you've
picked a type) so we can match it up. The recipient redeems it in person, by showing the code.
